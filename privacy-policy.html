Nexus Academy Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy for Nexus Academy: Pega CSA Practice Simulator

Effective Date: October 29, 2026

Developer: TechnoNexus Inc. ("we", "our", or "us")

1. Overview

Nexus Academy ("the App") is an educational exam preparation and scenario simulation utility designed to help professionals prepare for the Pega Certified System Architect (CSA) certification. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding user data.

2. Zero Personal Data Collection

Nexus Academy does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal identity information. Specifically:

  • No account creation or registration is required.
  • No name, email address, phone number, or physical address is gathered.
  • No device identifiers, IP addresses, or location data are logged or sent to any remote servers.
  • No advertising identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID) are tracked.

3. Local Storage on Device

All user progress, completed practice exam attempts, question scores, bookmarks, and UI preferences (such as Dark/Light mode theme and randomized question order) are stored strictly and exclusively on your local device via local browser storage (WebView LocalStorage). This data never leaves your device and is never transmitted to us or any third party.

4. Third-Party Services and Analytics

The App does not include any third-party tracking SDKs, analytics frameworks, behavioral telemetry, or third-party advertisement networks.

5. Internet Access & Network Permissions

The App is designed to function 100% offline. All 240 questions, scenarios, and architectural explanations are bundled locally within the application package. No continuous internet connection is required to take mock exams or view explanations.

6. Children’s Privacy

The App is intended for students and professionals. Because the App collects zero personal information, it complies with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be reflected with a revised effective date on this page.
